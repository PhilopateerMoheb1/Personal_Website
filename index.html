How an engagement runs

The same sequence works for a first assessment or a full certification program.

  1. Assess

    Understand scope, assets and current controls. Identify gaps and threats.

  2. Plan

    Prioritize risks and agree a treatment plan with clear owners and timelines.

  3. Implement

    Write policies and procedures, set up controls, and train the people who run them.

  4. Verify

    Audit internally, fix findings, and support the external audit.
